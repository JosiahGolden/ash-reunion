Welcome, Family

Come Home
to the Woods

✦

Some families have annual reunions. Ours takes over a whole state park. Every so often, the Ash family gathers — from across the country, across generations, and across every life stage imaginable — to do what we do best: laugh a little too loud, eat a little too much, stay up a little too late, and remind ourselves why family is everything.

This summer, we're heading to Pine Hills Group Camp at Lincoln State Park in Indiana, where there's a lake to swim in, woods to wander, and enough shaded porches for every good conversation that needs to happen. Whether you're driving an hour or flying across the country, we're saving you a seat at the table.

Come hungry. Come ready. Come home.

Where You'll Sleep

Accommodations

The main group camp is covered — but there are extra options if you want your own space.

🏕️

Pine Hills Group Camp

The siblings have got this covered — the entire group camp is reserved for our family. With capacity for up to 155 people, there's room for everyone. Shared facilities, lake access, and a beach area are all included.

Up to 155 people Check-in Thu 3 PM Checkout Sun 12 PM

On-Site Cabins

A limited number of private cabins are available to rent individually through Lincoln State Park. These book fast — the minimum rental period is 7 days starting on a Saturday, so they start the Saturday before our reunion weekend.

Available to book RIGHT NOW. Visit the Indiana State Park reservations portal to check availability.

⚡ Book Now 7-night minimum Starts Saturday

RV & Tent Sites

Prefer your own home-away-from-home? Individual RV hookup sites and primitive tent camping spots are available within Lincoln State Park. These can be reserved through the Indiana State Park reservations system.

Reservations open in January. Mark your calendar!

Things To Do

Lincoln State Park is surrounded by some of Indiana's best attractions. There's something for every age.

🎢

Holiday World & Splashin' Safari

A beloved Indiana landmark — world-class roller coasters, one of the top water parks in the country, and free soft drinks all day long. Worth every minute.

📍 ~8 min · Santa Claus, IN
🏖️

Lake Lincoln & Beach

A beautiful sandy beach right on-site at Lincoln State Park. Swimming, kayaking, paddleboats, and fishing all within walking distance of camp.

📍 On-site
🎅

Town of Santa Claus, IN

The one and only — a charming little town with a big personality. Visit the Santa Claus Museum, grab ice cream, and mail a letter with that legendary postmark.

📍 ~8 min · Santa Claus, IN
🥾

Park Hiking Trails

Lincoln State Park has miles of wooded trails winding through southern Indiana forest. Great for morning walks, bird watching, and quiet moments away from the crowd.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked

Things people actually want to know. More details will be added as the reunion gets closer.

Yes — Lincoln State Park has parking areas near the group camp. We'll share detailed parking instructions closer to the event, including where to pull in and where to unload before parking. Carpooling from nearby is encouraged if you're at a hotel or cabin outside the park.

We'll share a full packing list before the reunion, but the essentials: sunscreen, bug spray, comfortable shoes for the outdoors, a swimsuit and towel for the lake, a lawn chair, and layers for cool evenings. If you have a favorite game or activity to share with the family, bring it!

Full meal details are still being finalized! The plan is to have shared group meals covered as part of the reunion. We'll send a complete breakdown — including any meals you might want to supplement on your own — in the planning communications before the event.

Lincoln State Park does allow leashed pets in many areas, but group camp facilities may have specific rules. Please check with the reunion organizers before planning to bring a pet, so we can confirm the group camp policy and make sure everyone has a comfortable stay.

Cell service at Lincoln State Park can be limited depending on your carrier, and there is no guaranteed Wi-Fi at the group camp. Consider this your built-in excuse to put the phone down, be present with family, and reconnect with the people who matter most. We'll make sure everything important is communicated before you arrive.

The nearest hospital is Memorial Hospital in Jasper, Indiana (~25 minutes away). There are also urgent care clinics in the surrounding area. We'll include a full list of nearby medical facilities in the pre-reunion information packet for your peace of mind.
