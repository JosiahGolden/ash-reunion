Welcome, Family

Come Home
to the Woods

✦

Audie and Mary raised eight children together — Cindy, Cheryl, Chester, David, Eldon, Teresa, Valerie, and Forest — and this reunion brings all their families back under one roof. Or in this case, fifteen cottages in the Indiana woods.

Every so often the Ash family gathers — from across the country, across generations, and across every life stage — to do what we do best: laugh a little too loud, eat a little too much, stay up way too late, and remind ourselves why family is everything.

This summer we're at Pine Hills Group Camp at Lincoln State Park, where there's a lake to swim in, woods to wander, and enough porch space for every good conversation that needs to happen. Whether you're driving an hour or flying across the country, we're saving you a seat at the table.

Come hungry. Come ready. Come home.

Explore the Area

Things To Do

Lincoln State Park is surrounded by some of Indiana's best attractions. There's something for every age.

🎢

Holiday World & Splashin' Safari

A beloved Indiana landmark — world-class roller coasters, one of the top water parks in the country, and free soft drinks all day long. Worth every minute.

📍 ~8 min · Santa Claus, IN
🏖️

Lake & Beach

The lake is open for swimming and kayaking — bring your kayak if you have one! No motorized boats at this time. Water levels may affect availability, so we'll keep everyone posted.

📍 On-site · Lincoln State Park
🎅

Town of Santa Claus, IN

The one and only — a charming little town with a big personality. Visit the Santa Claus Museum, grab ice cream, and mail a letter with that legendary postmark.

📍 ~8 min · Santa Claus, IN
🥾

Park Hiking Trails

Lincoln State Park has miles of wooded trails winding through southern Indiana forest. Great for morning walks, bird watching, and quiet moments away from the crowd.

📍 On-site · Lincoln State Park
🏐

Field Games

Volleyball net, kickball, wiffle ball, cornhole (we're bringing it), and disc golf on the schedule. Full activity lineup and any tournament details coming soon.

📍 On-site · Group Camp
🎣

Fishing at Weber Lake

Fishing available at nearby Weber Lake. A valid fishing license is required — out-of-state licenses are $15 for a 1-day pass or $35 for a 7-day pass.

📍 Nearby · Weber Lake

Good Questions

Frequently Asked

Things people actually want to know. More details will be added as the reunion gets closer.

The entrance fee is a one-time charge per vehicle — not per day. Stop at the front gate when you arrive to pay and pick up your parking pass.

Indiana residents$7 one time
Out-of-state$15 one time

36 vehicles from our group park by the cottages — those spots are included in the rental. Any additional vehicles are welcome to park by the dining hall. There is no limit on parking passes.

🏡 Group Cottages

  • 1–2 queen sheet sets (depending on cottage size)
  • Twin sheet sets — 8 to 12 sets depending on beds utilized
  • Plastic mattress covers (if preferred)
  • Pillows and blankets
  • Toiletries & flip flops for showers
  • Food for 2 breakfasts, 2 lunches, and snacks
  • Bathing suits and towels for the lake
  • Garbage bags, cleaning supplies, dish soap

🪵 Rented Cabins (individual rental)

  • Pillows are provided — bring your own pillowcases
  • Bring all bed linens
  • Toiletries
  • Cabin has a stove, refrigerator, and pots & pans
  • Bring your own silverware and plates

🌊 For the Lake

  • Bathing suits and towels
  • Sunscreen and bug spray
  • Kayak (if you have one — lake is open to kayaks!)
  • Fishing gear + license if you plan to fish

There are two group dinners provided for everyone in the dining hall: a BBQ cookout on Friday at 5 PM and a spaghetti dinner on Saturday at 4 PM. All other meals — breakfasts, lunches, and snacks for the full weekend — are on your own. Plan to bring enough food for two days plus a little extra.

The dining hall is well-equipped for our group meals. Here's what's there and what you'll need to bring:

✅ Provided

  • Industrial stove with double oven
  • Warming table
  • Dishwasher
  • Pots, pans, baking sheets, serving spoons & utensils
  • Walk-in refrigerator and freezer
  • Coffee pot
  • Toilet paper for shower and bath houses

🛍️ Please Bring

  • Dish soap (for the dishwasher)
  • Garbage bags
  • Cleaning supplies

Note: there is no microwave in the dining hall.

No pets are permitted in the group cottage area. Please make arrangements for your pets before the trip. We want everyone — two-legged and four-legged — to have the best possible experience, and this policy keeps things comfortable for all guests.

Public Wi-Fi is available at certain spots in the park — including near the nature center and the dining hall area. The cottages themselves don't have private Wi-Fi, so consider this a good excuse to put the phone down, be present with family, and soak up the weekend. Everything important will be communicated before you arrive.

The nearest hospital is Memorial Hospital in Jasper, Indiana (~25 minutes away). There are also urgent care clinics in the surrounding area. We'll include a full list of nearby medical facilities in the final pre-reunion information sent to all families.
